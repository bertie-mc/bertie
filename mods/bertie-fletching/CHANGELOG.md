# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial stable release, included in Bertie's client and server packs.

- Eight-arrow crafts with separate extra and potion inputs, a persistent potion tank, and combined arrow visuals.
- Sixty-four arrow components with custom flight, damage, status, and utility effects.
- Consistent bow-critical damage, shared Sun/Echo critical hits, constrained homing, and bounded secondary chains.
- Ingredient-based material colors, outline slot hints, component search tags, and revised descriptions.

Gameplay matches the tested 0.2.1 build. Existing redesigned arrows and potion tanks remain compatible.
