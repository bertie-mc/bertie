# Chemical progression and acquisition

Chemical Oxidizer has the captured 5×5 Nether Table recipe. Quantum Entangloporter
uses the revised full 7×7 End Table layout and produces two. Exact layouts live in
`texture-work/queued_051_layouts.json`; the selected post-pass is
`texture-work/queued_051_recipes.py`.

Arcane Anvil is a Spirit Altar operation: one Anvil, sixteen Amethyst Blocks,
four Large Diamond Slates and four Polished Deepslate, with sixteen Arcane,
fifteen Earthen and eight Aqueous Spirits. Its manual crafting route is replaced.
Spirit registry identifiers retain the `malum:` namespace.

Pigment Extractor and Mixer use the red Infused Alloy in place of Thermonuclear
Alloy, preserving the other ingredients and counts. Molten Thermonuclear's PRC
operation uses one Sculkium Ingot in place of Netherite; fluids, chemical amounts,
duration and energy remain unchanged.

The five large machines with native Miniaturization routes no longer have their
crafting shortcuts: Heat Generator, Gas-Burning Generator, Pigment Mixer,
Chemical Infuser and Electrolytic Separator. The other large machines retain
their crafting routes while their Mini structures remain unselected.

The quest book adds Oxidizer between Osmium Compressor and Radiance Dust, keeping
the Refined Glowstone material prerequisite. Super AI Chip leads through Chemical
Dissolution Chamber, Isotopic Centrifuge and the six-part Fission Reactor quest.
Turbine Parts branches above Reactor; Enriched Thermonuclear sits up-right and
Plutonium Pellet continues the horizontal line. Turbine Parts requires one of
each of its ten native construction components.

Arcane Anvil is in C2 above Arcane Ingot, requiring Spirit Altar. Buffs no longer
contains its old entry or either special grindstone. Enriched Materials is removed.
Mekanism/Oritech reference tabs retain processing progression; optional utility
and energy-option catalogues are withdrawn. Integrated processing objectives use
checkmarks. Main progression quests and all unselected live positions remain.
