# End eye acquisition

The retained twelve-eye route replaces the original acquisition methods for
the eyes below.

| Eye | Source |
|---|---|
| Trial | 1% per Ominous Vault reward |
| Old | 2% when a Suspicious Sand block's archaeology loot is generated |
| Sculk | Sculk Leech 0.1%; Snapper 0.5%; Centipede 1%; Shattered 4%; Stalker 8%; Shriek Worm 15% |
| Cryptic | 3% after a successful enchant with a required level of at least 80, including Apothic/Raven tables |
| Magical | A successful mana-consuming spell leaves exactly zero mana while Eye of Ender is held offhand; one input eye becomes Magical Eye |
| Cursed | Eye of Ender held by an adult/baby Piglin during zombification becomes Cursed Eye; it always drops on death |
| Cold | Igloo chest 10%; Aquamirae Frozen Chest 17%; snowy village house chest 0.5% |
| Nether | 7% from Wither, Zoranth the Forgotten One, Ignis, Netherite Monstrosity, Helvar and Echo of Tyros |
| Evil | Wandering Trader: 40% actual-offer chance, 16–64 Emeralds. Level-4 Cleric: 30%, 64 Emerald Coins |
| Corrupted | One eye drops at the raid centre when the raid transitions to victory |
| Exotic | Buried Treasure 5%; successful Tropical Fish feeding of a Dolphin 20% |
| Lost | Mineshaft and village weaponsmith/toolsmith/armorer chests: 1% each |

Sculk death rolls ignore Looting and are absent from EMI's loot-table previews.
Magical Eye checks actual mana immediately after payment. Free casts and rounded
zero displays do not qualify; the old Evoker and Mansion sources are disabled.
The eye remains visible. Other chest loot is retained, and old eye injections
are replaced rather than added to the new chances. A raid already in its
victory state on reload does not award another eye.

Piglins can pick up Eye of Ender. Conversion preserves it through vanilla's
admired-item cleanup and assigns the transformed held item a guaranteed drop.
The Cursed Eye lore refers to Piglin corruption; Corrupted Eye is the separate
raid reward. The Cleric offer requires the optional `bertiecoins:emerald_coin`
item and is not generated while that currency is unavailable.

Potato, Fishing, Witch, Undead, Guardian, Rogue, Wither and Black Eyes, plus
Undead Soul, are withdrawn from recipes, loot, functional tags, creative tabs,
EMI and the quest list. The Stronghold objective requires the twelve retained
eye objectives; Trial and Sculk replace the obsolete objectives.
