# Changelog

## 0.53.1

- Keep Solerium, Corronium and cube-shaped Plutonium usable under Bertie
  Progression, with their native item/ore/block forms and licensed artwork.
- Restore Corronium/Solerium processing forms, slurries and recipes while keeping
  Desh, Neptunium and Tharsite retired. Corronium's former byproduct chain contracts to Eezo.
- Alias former Stellaris material IDs to the new entries for surviving saved
  stacks and blocks; Stellaris and its addons remain removed.

## 0.53.0

- Retire Stellaris and its dedicated addons, removing the five exclusive metal
  processing families, 45 custom items and ten slurry registrations.
- Preserve shared metals and fuels; remove obsolete processing routes and dust
  eligibility whose only ore provider was Stellaris.
- Add the charging/tool quest branches and one combined C3 Station Seed/emitter
  quest; remove Cybernetics' Electron Tube prerequisite while preserving live layouts.

## 0.52.1

- Resolve Forgified Fabric API's dynamic packet codec on newer NeoForge return
  branches, fixing Do a Barrel Roll configuration sync disconnects during world join.

## 0.52.0

- Add the two-input Create Mechanical Adapter and Copper Casing construction for
  the Augmentation Center; close its external RF charging and export routes.
- Replace Auto-Feeder with Adaptive Immune System for Unbound vacuum protection;
  exchange Living Fortress and Flight in the research tree.
- Apply the captured powered-tool, charging-station, Quantum Research Station
  and two Elytra recipes; move Mechanical Fusion Anvil to anvil compression.
- Use Titanium Sheets in Quantum Entangloporter and Fire Dragonsteel in
  thermonuclear production; accept either personal container in Chemical Oxidizer.
- Retire the two Rustic Gliders and Speed Booster; add the selected bulk/material
  quest links and relocate the selected entries while preserving live layouts.
- Keep End Remastered loot-codec aliases and owo's sided networking compatible
  with the NeoForge version required by Unbound.

## 0.51.0

- Repair the EMI Ponder button; add material-icon step navigation, name tooltips
  and angled previews through both EMI and the normal Ponder shortcut.
- Flatten Sentient Casing faces while retaining animated purple emissive materials.
- Move Arcane Anvil acquisition to Spirit Altar; add the captured Chemical Oxidizer
  and two-output Quantum Entangloporter crafts.
- Use Sculkium for thermonuclear production and Infused Alloy for both pigment
  machines; remove the five large-machine crafting shortcuts covered by Mini recipes.
- Add the chemical/fission/turbine/material quest branches, relocate Arcane Anvil
  to C2 and remove selected Buffs and already-integrated reference entries.
- Preserve manually arranged quest positions through guarded field-level updates.
